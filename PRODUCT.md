# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

delegated: plain HTML/CSS/JS served by a zero-dependency Node server (server.js), catalog synced from the Printful API. Chosen so Daniele can run it anywhere with only Node installed.

## Users

Young adults (roughly 18-35, Italy first) who buy a funny shirt to wear on a night out, or as a gag gift for a friend. They arrive from social posts on their phone, decide in seconds, and buy because a line made them laugh. Two sections: Uomo and Donna.

## Product Purpose

Your Favorite Daddy Shop sells print-on-demand t-shirts printed with cheeky pickup lines ("da rimorchio", e.g. "Call me daddy", "Who's your daddy tonight?"). Every shirt is printed and shipped by Printful only when ordered. Success: a visitor laughs at a line, picks a size, and checks out.

## Positioning

The line is the product. The shirt exists to deliver a pickup line out loud without having to say it.

## Operating Context

Phone-first browsing from Instagram/TikTok links, often in the evening. Fulfilment, production and shipping (3-7 working days) by Printful, store "Your Favorite Daddy Shop" (id 15135959). Orders and customers managed in /admin. Online payment not yet active (orders are confirmed by contact); Stripe is the planned next step.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Only designs actually present in Daniele's Printful store/templates are sold.
- Prices come from Printful data plus Daniele's overrides (data/catalog-overrides.json); default 29,90 EUR, larger sizes cost more.
- Language: Italian site copy; the lines themselves may be English or Italian.
- Cheekiness level 1-5 ("sfacciataggine") is a site concept per design.
- Undecided: final domain, payment provider, shipping prices.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: Your Favorite Daddy Shop.
- Voice: ironic, flirtatious, self-aware, never vulgar or demeaning. Footer note that consent is not a joke stays.
- Daniele approved the current copy and lines (10 Oct 2026); he rejected the first visual style (blush pink + wine, Shrikhand) as weak.

## Evidence on Hand

- Printful store: 1 published product ("Who's Your Daddy Tonight?", base discontinued), 8 saved templates, mockups on files.cdn.printful.com.
- No reviews, testimonials, press or sales numbers exist: never invent them.

## Product Principles

1. The line comes first, everywhere: biggest thing on every screen.
2. Decide in seconds on a phone: line, price, size, add.
3. Funny, not cheap: the craft makes the joke land.
4. Honest commerce: real prices, real shipping times, no fake urgency or fake reviews.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA contrast, keyboard operable cart and checkout, reduced-motion respected.
